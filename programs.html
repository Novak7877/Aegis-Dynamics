How a program comes to market.

From initial concept to fielded system, every Aegis Dynamics program follows the same four-stage lifecycle.

01

Concept & Feasibility

Requirements gathered from allied government partners, assessed against current technical and regulatory feasibility.

02

Development & Testing

Prototype development, field testing, and iterative refinement under live operational conditions.

03

Certification & Approval

Compliance review against export control frameworks and client-nation procurement standards.

04

Production & Fielding

Full-rate production, delivery, and long-term sustainment support across the program's service life.
