Six decades of building what nations can't build alone.

Aegis Dynamics was founded in Canada in 1961 by four executives from the private defense sector. Six decades of acquisitions and organic growth have made it one of the largest defense conglomerates in the world.

The group now spans armament, cybersecurity, and exoskeleton technology, operating through more than sixty subsidiaries and joint ventures. Our programs are delivered under the compliance frameworks of every government we serve.

We build in the open. What each government does with what we build is not ours to disclose.
